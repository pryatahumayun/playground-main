# Tools

This section is not about memorizing every tool feature. It is about knowing what types of work these tools support in integrated delivery environments.

## Jira

Often used for:

- backlog management
- story tracking
- sprint planning
- defect tracking

## Azure DevOps

Often used for:

- boards and work tracking
- repos and pipelines
- test plans
- release workflows

## Enterprise Architect

Often used for:

- UML modeling
- system diagrams
- architecture artifacts
- traceability between model elements

## IBM Jazz

Often used in enterprise or regulated environments for:

- requirements
- lifecycle management
- traceability
- testing and change coordination

## Interview Phrases

- `I think of these tools as part of the evidence trail around requirements, design, testing, and change.`
- `The exact product matters less than understanding what capability it supports in the delivery lifecycle.`
