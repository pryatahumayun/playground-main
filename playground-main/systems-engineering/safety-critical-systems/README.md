# Safety-Critical Systems

Safety-critical systems are systems where failure can cause major operational, human, regulatory, or mission impact.

## Why They Matter

These environments place more emphasis on:

- controlled change
- documentation
- validation evidence
- reliability
- predictability

## Common Characteristics

- formal requirements
- traceability
- strict testing and validation
- change approval rigor
- incident and recovery discipline

## Example

In a safety-sensitive environment, a new feature is not just judged on whether it works in dev. It may need:

- reviewed requirements
- integration validation
- evidence of testing
- rollback or recovery planning
- clear transition to operations

## Interview Phrases

- `In safety-sensitive environments, predictability and validation are often more important than speed of change.`
- `I would expect stronger traceability from requirement to implementation to test evidence.`
- `Operational impact and failure mode analysis matter much more in these environments.`
