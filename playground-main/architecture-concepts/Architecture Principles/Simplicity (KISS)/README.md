# Simplicity (KISS)

KISS means "keep it simple" and is one of the healthiest architecture instincts.

## What To Know

- simple designs are easier to explain, operate, and debug
- complexity should be earned by real requirements
- premature architecture often creates more problems than it solves

## Good Interview Points

- choose the least complex design that meets the requirements
- start simple and evolve when evidence demands it
- simplicity improves maintainability and reliability
