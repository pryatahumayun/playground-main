# Maintainability

Maintainability is how easy a system is to understand, change, test, and support over time.

## What To Know

- naming, documentation, and modular structure matter
- tightly coupled systems are harder to maintain
- automation reduces operational burden

## Good Interview Points

- simpler designs are often easier to maintain than clever ones
- CI/CD and automated testing improve safe change velocity
- observability and runbooks make operations more maintainable
