# Capability Maps

Capability maps show what the business does, independent of org chart, current systems, or individual teams.

## Why capability maps are useful

- they help separate "what the business needs" from "how it is currently implemented"
- they make prioritization easier
- they help connect investment decisions to business value

## Common uses

- strategy and portfolio planning
- application rationalization
- identifying capability gaps
- aligning technology initiatives to business outcomes

## Good to remember

- a capability map is not the same thing as a process map
- capabilities should stay stable even when org structures or systems change
