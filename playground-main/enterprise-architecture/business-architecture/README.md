# Business Architecture

Business architecture connects strategy, operating model, business capabilities, and value delivery.

## Common topics

- business capabilities
- value streams
- operating model
- organization and stakeholder views
- process maps
- business outcomes and priorities

## Questions it answers

- What does the business need to be good at?
- Which capabilities are strategic?
- How does value move from idea to customer outcome?
- Where are the organizational bottlenecks?

## Why it matters

Business architecture gives context for why applications, data, and technology changes are happening in the first place.
