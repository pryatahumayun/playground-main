# Architecture Principles

Architecture principles are the high-level rules that guide design decisions across the enterprise.

## Examples of principles

- prefer managed services when they meet the need
- design for resilience and recoverability
- secure by default
- treat APIs as products
- keep data ownership clear
- automate infrastructure and deployments

## Why they matter

Principles help teams make consistent decisions even when detailed standards do not yet exist.

## Good principle characteristics

- easy to understand
- broad enough to reuse
- specific enough to guide real tradeoffs
- backed by a rationale, not just a slogan
